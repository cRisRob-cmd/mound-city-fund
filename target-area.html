Definition

The Fund's target area consists of the neighborhoods of North St. Louis City, generally the area of the City of St. Louis lying north of Delmar Boulevard, together with adjacent low- to moderate-income census tracts as designated by HUD. Priority is given to projects located in the following neighborhoods: Academy, Baden, College Hill, Fairground, Greater Ville, Hamilton Heights, Hyde Park, JeffVanderLou, Kingsway East, Kingsway West, Mark Twain, North Pointe, O'Fallon, Penrose, The Ville, Visitation Park, Walnut Park East, Walnut Park West, Wells Goodfellow, and West End. A property outside these named neighborhoods remains eligible if it is located within the target area as defined above; the selection committee applies the boundary, not the neighborhood label.

Adjacent LMI Census Tracts

In addition to the North City neighborhoods shown above, properties in census tracts adjacent to the target area qualify where the tract is designated low- to moderate-income under HUD's published data. Applicants uncertain whether a property qualifies may submit the address with a pre-application, and Fund staff will confirm threshold eligibility within ten business days, as provided in Section 6 of the Grant Program Guidelines.

Published under Section 3 of the Grant Program Guidelines. The Board of Directors may amend the target area at any time; the version posted on this website governs applications submitted after posting.
