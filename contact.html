Contact the Fund

Homeowners, donors, lenders, contractors, and community organizations all have a role in this work. We want to hear from you.

Grant Applicants

If you are a homeowner interested in applying, start by reading the Grant Program Guidelines and reviewing the target area. To request a pre-application form or ask an eligibility question, email grants@moundcityfund.org. We confirm threshold eligibility within ten business days of receiving a pre-application.

Donors and Funders

For questions about giving, gift acknowledgment, restricted gifts, or grants from donor-advised funds, foundations, and corporate giving programs, email give@moundcityfund.org or see For Donors. Checks may be mailed to the address below.

Lenders, Agencies, and Community Partners

We are built to stack with the programs already working in North St. Louis, and we welcome referrals from lenders, CDFIs, city agencies, and neighborhood organizations. To discuss a referral pipeline, a stacking structure, or a partnership, email partners@moundcityfund.org.

Governance and Document Requests

Questions regarding governance, and requests for documents available under our Public Disclosure Statement, may be directed to info@moundcityfund.org or to the mailing address below. Good-faith reports under our Whistleblower Policy may be made to the Board President or any other officer or director, and may be made anonymously.
