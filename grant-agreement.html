Residential Redevelopment Grant Agreement (Template)

The agreement executed by every approved grantee before disbursement, published so applicants know its terms in advance.

This Grant Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of ______________ (the "Effective Date") by and between Mound City Fund, a Missouri nonprofit corporation (the "Fund"), and ______________________________ ("Grantee"), with respect to the residential property located at ______________________________, St. Louis, Missouri (the "Property").

1. Grant

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Fund awards Grantee a charitable grant equal to ______% of Qualified Redevelopment Costs actually incurred for the approved project, not to exceed $______________ (the "Grant"). "Qualified Redevelopment Costs" means the costs identified in the approved Scope of Work attached as Exhibit A, as defined in the Fund's Grant Program Guidelines, which are incorporated by reference. The Grant is charitable assistance in furtherance of the Fund's exempt purposes and is not a loan, an investment, or compensation for services.

2. Disbursement

The Grant will be disbursed in tranches upon Grantee's satisfaction of the milestones set out in Exhibit B, verified by inspection or by documentation reasonably acceptable to the Fund (contractor invoices, receipts, permits, and lien waivers). At the Fund's election, any tranche may be paid directly to Grantee's licensed contractor. The Fund has no obligation to disburse any amount while Grantee is in breach of this Agreement or if the representations in Section 4 cease to be true.

3. Use of Funds; Project Completion

Grantee shall use Grant proceeds solely for Qualified Redevelopment Costs under the approved Scope of Work, shall obtain all required permits, shall use licensed and insured contractors for all work requiring licensure, and shall complete the project by ______________, subject to extensions granted in writing for good cause. Grantee shall permit the Fund and its representatives reasonable access to the Property, on reasonable notice, to verify progress and completion.

4. Representations of Grantee

Grantee represents and warrants that: (a) Grantee holds legal title to the Property or will hold title at or before the first disbursement, free of undisclosed liens; (b) all information in Grantee's application is true, correct, and complete in all material respects; (c) Grantee is current on real estate taxes for the Property or enrolled in an approved payment plan; (d) Grantee is not a director, officer, employee, or substantial contributor of the Fund, nor a family member or controlled entity of any of them; and (e) if Grantee qualified as a low- to moderate-income applicant, the household income documentation provided is accurate.

5. Occupancy Commitment and Recapture

If Grantee is an owner-occupant, Grantee agrees to occupy the Property as Grantee's principal residence for thirty-six (36) months following project completion (the "Occupancy Period"). If Grantee sells, transfers, or ceases to occupy the Property during the Occupancy Period, Grantee shall repay a prorated portion of the Grant, reduced by one thirty-sixth for each full month of completed occupancy. If Grantee is a developer, the community-benefit conditions set out in Exhibit C apply in place of this Section. The Fund may, in its discretion, waive recapture in cases of death, disability, involuntary relocation, or comparable hardship.

6. Default and Remedies

Upon Grantee's material misuse of funds, material misrepresentation, or failure to complete the project as required, the Fund may suspend further disbursements, terminate this Agreement, and demand repayment of amounts disbursed. The Fund's remedies are cumulative, and its forbearance is not a waiver.

7. Miscellaneous

Grantee may not assign this Agreement without the Fund's written consent. This Agreement, with its exhibits, is the entire agreement of the parties concerning the Grant, may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties, and is governed by the laws of the State of Missouri. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or agency between the parties. Grantee acknowledges that the Fund has made no representation regarding the tax treatment of the Grant and that Grantee is responsible for its own tax obligations. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement remains in effect.
